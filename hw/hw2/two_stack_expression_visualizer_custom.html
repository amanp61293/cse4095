Rules

  1. Push numbers onto the numbers stack.
  2. Push ( onto the operators stack.
  3. If + or - appears where an operand is expected, treat it as a unary operator.
  4. Unary + and unary - have higher precedence than * and /.
  5. Before pushing a binary operator, apply operators of higher or equal precedence.
  6. For ), apply operators until the matching ( is reached.
  7. At the end, apply all remaining operators.
